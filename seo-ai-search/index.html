WHAT I LOOK AT

01 / FOUNDATION

Can Google reach it?

Crawling, indexing, page structure, internal links and the technical problems hiding good work.

02 / DEMAND

What are buyers asking?

Map commercial questions to pages and product paths, without filling the site with interchangeable articles.

03 / AI ANSWERS

Who gets named or cited?

Use a defined set of prompts and repeated checks to see what AI answers use, then trace the source pages.

04 / OUTCOME

Did anything move?

Track dated changes against Search Console, search results and on-site actions. Keep impressions, clicks and revenue separate.
