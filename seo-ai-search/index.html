01 / CAPTURE DEMAND

Capture the demand already there.

Commercial and technical SEO, page architecture, internal links and landing pages put you in front of buyers who know what they need.

02 / NEW SURFACES

Show up where buyers ask for recommendations.

Map comparison questions, AI answers and the sources they use. Improve retrievability and entity clarity, then test actual mentions and citations.
