# Purple Cow editorial pass: The Build Log

Applied the [Purple Cow Marketing skill](https://github.com/guia-matthieu/clawfu-skills/blob/main/skills/content/purple-cow-marketing/SKILL.md) to the three MDX review drafts on 3 October 2026. The test is whether a reader can repeat the idea and point to something visible. It is an editorial judgment, not evidence of word of mouth.

| Draft | Sentence a reader could repeat | Visible proof | Who would pass it along | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI search is not one chart | "He measures AI appearances, referred visits and product use separately." | Three-signal visual; planned GSC and PostHog screenshots | SaaS growth leads tired of vague visibility scores | The Pepys figure requires a fresh PostHog export before publication. |
| What does an MVP cost? | "He prices the first job the product must finish, then cuts everything else." | Scope ladder and planned annotated scope worksheet | Founders comparing build paths | This is a scoping view, not an industry price benchmark. |
| QuoteSweep went from 96 to 988 Google clicks a month | "A closed-beta insurance product grew Google discovery tenfold, and he shows what the graph does not prove." | Five-month chart with units and source | Vertical SaaS founders, search leads | GSC exports, query/page split and dated change log still need review. |

## What stayed out

- No claim that AI citations caused Pepys signups or QuoteSweep clicks.
- No invented MVP project cost, client result, or causal SEO tactic.
- No mass-produced glossary post built only to match a high-volume term.
- No novelty in the title without a concrete visual or decision inside the article.

## Humanizer pass

The local humanizer skill was used as an editorial checklist and analyzer. It flagged repeated bold lead-ins in the MVP draft; those were rewritten as sentences. The final analyzer returned no matched pattern for all three drafts. That score is a copyediting signal, not proof that the writing is good or that it will rank.
