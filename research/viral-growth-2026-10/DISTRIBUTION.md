## Week 3: Pepys AI search measurement

**Graphic:** `assets/02-pepys-referrals.png`. PostHog rule and counts verified 4 October 2026. Use this when the AI measurement article is approved for publication; until then it can be shared without a case-file link if Ankur approves the copy.

**Post copy:**

> Pepys had 8.6× as many ChatGPT-referred sessions in September as in July.
>
> It's tempting to call that “8.6× more AI citations.” It isn't.
>
> PostHog counted 190 sessions in July, 948 in August and 1,637 in September using the same rule: Pepys entry page, ChatGPT referring domain and at least one pageview.
>
> That tells me people arrived from ChatGPT. It doesn't tell me which answer sent them, how many times Pepys was mentioned, or whether they used the product. I track appearances, referrals, observed citations and product actions separately.
>
> What are you actually measuring when you say AI search is growing?

**Alt text:** “Bar chart of Pepys sessions with a pageview and a ChatGPT entry referrer: July 190, August 948, September 1,637. September is 8.6 times July.”

**Post copy:**

> Before I price or build an MVP, I want four answers on one page.
>
> Who is the first user? What job must they finish? What would prove it worked? And what can wait?
>
> The fourth answer usually does the most work. It turns an impressive feature list into a first release someone can actually use.
>
> I made a small scope card to use with a cofounder or team. Fill it in before the next “what if we also added...” conversation.
