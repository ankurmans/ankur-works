# Landing-page copy

Live draft: [mascot-branding/index.html](../../mascot-branding/index.html). This file records the copy structure and the intent behind it.

**Eyebrow:** Character-led marketing / for product brands

**H1:** Give your product a face people want to see again

**Explainer:** I make brand mascots, give them a point of view, then put them to work in product launches, films and content people can recognise.

**Primary CTA:** Build my character. **Secondary CTA:** See the characters.

**Proof 1:** Meet Quincy. A QR code with a personality. Whooshly's sticker mascot works as a product guide and a recurring face for links and QR codes, including in a short launch film.

**Proof 2:** A scribe with more than one line. Pepys's little scribe can move from a review to a language feature without losing its personality.

**Turn:** Your logo identifies you. Your character can do things.

**System:** Find its face → give it rules → put it in motion → bring it back.

**Offer:** Character sprint → Launch kit → Content engine.

**Founder link:** Made by a builder who has to market his own products.

**Close:** Tell me what your brand does. I'll think about who it could be.

Copy exclusions: no unapproved prospect name or reaction; no invented engagement, views, conversion or branded-search result; no promise of viral content or IP clearance; no claim that Ankur is a formal design agency.
