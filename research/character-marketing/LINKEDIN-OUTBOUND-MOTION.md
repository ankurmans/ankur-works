# LinkedIn and outbound motion — draft only

Start with product founders where a character could explain an actual product job: SaaS/AI tools, creator products, visually abstract B2B categories, or brands with a dormant mascot. Research each product first. A character sketch without a product truth is decoration.

Suggested sequence:

1. Shortlist 15 prospects using public product and founder pages. Record why each product has a character-sized explanation problem.
2. Make one **clearly labelled speculative concept** per prospect: one pose, one product-specific scene and a brief note about where it would be used.
3. Send a personal note such as "I made a character concept for [product] that explains [specific feature]. Want to see it?" only after the user authorises outreach. Do not claim commission or relationship.
4. Share the concept privately first. Obtain written permission before public posting, case-study use, logo use or quoting feedback.
5. Track reply, meeting, qualified brief and project stage. Do not treat impressions as pipeline.

No outreach has been sent as part of this build. The unapproved prospect is excluded from proof and publishing until permission is documented.
