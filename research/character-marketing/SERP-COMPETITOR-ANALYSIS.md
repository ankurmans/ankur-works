## Page implications

1. Put finished characters and film next to the headline, then show a product use case for each.
2. Use "brand mascot" and "mascot branding" in natural metadata and copy; lead with "character-led marketing" as the editorial category.
3. State plainly that this is for product and founder-led brands, not costume production or school teams.
4. Show the character-to-content path and a clear direct-to-Ankur CTA. No outcome guarantee or invented analytics.
5. Treat galleries and "best mascots" content as a later editorial test only if Ankur can add original analysis and safe image rights.
