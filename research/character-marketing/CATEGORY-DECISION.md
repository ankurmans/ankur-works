# Category decision

Decision date: 2026-10-03. Data source: [DataForSEO keyword rows](DATAFORSEO-KEYWORDS.csv), [US desktop SERPs](SERP-COMPETITOR-ANALYSIS.md), and [editorial scorecard](OFFER-NAMING-SCORECARD.csv).

| Role | Choice | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Positioning name | **Character-led marketing** | Best fit for creating, codifying and repeatedly using a character across product content. It scored 87/110 on an editorial rubric; the score is judgment, not measured market data. |
| Plain-English descriptor | **Brand mascots, launch films and recurring product content** | Buyers can understand the outputs in one sentence. |
| Primary SEO family | **Mascot branding** with natural support for **brand mascot** and **brand mascot design** | US Google Ads returned 1,600 monthly for both `mascot branding` and `brand mascot`; these appear to be close variants and must not be added together. The `mascot branding` SERP had a relevant service result. |
| Route | **/mascot-branding/** | Short, readable and aligned with the broad query family. The hero immediately narrows it to product-brand characters. |

US volume snapshots: `mascot marketing` 50; `brand mascot design` 30; `brand character design` 10; `character marketing` 20. DataForSEO returned **no metric** for exact `character-led marketing`; that is not a zero-volume claim. UK/Canada/Australia are separate rows, not added to US. Google Ads can group close variants, and these are directional search estimates rather than buyers or leads.

The related-keyword expansion for `mascot branding` (US, depth 1) returned examples/ideas/template and broader famous-mascot queries. Those support an original analysis roadmap, not extra service-page volume. Keyword suggestions were screened for costume and school intent. Autocomplete was not queried because its endpoint was not in the configured cost ledger, which requires a separate approval before spend. Blank autocomplete cells in the research CSV mean **not checked**, not no suggestions.

The core offer is **character system + product marketing + recurring content**. Pure mascot illustration would commoditise Ankur's contribution; "growth" would imply causal results not established by the current proof. The defensible distinction is that Ankur builds and markets products himself, and the page can show character work in the actual Pepys and Whooshly product worlds. [Mascot Branding](https://mascotbranding.com/) already offers character systems and videos, so differentiation must come from executed product context, voice and proof, not a novelty claim.

Costume, sports and school queries remain excluded. `mascot design agency` has commercial results but a school-costume result and a costume PAA. `character marketing` is ambiguous with unrelated agencies called Character. The route does not promise search rank; the offer can sell from LinkedIn and direct outreach while organic visibility develops.

Content authority bet: become known for **characters with a job in product marketing**. A concept, a film and repeatable scenes are stronger proof than an encyclopedia of famous mascots. No programmatic gallery pages until original analysis, image rights and SERP fit are shown.
