# Landing-page wireframe

1. **Hero, one screen:** left headline, one-sentence explanation and two CTAs; right Pepys’s full-body mascot beside a smaller Quincy. They arrive with a short entrance. No autoplay video payload.
2. **Visual proof immediately:** Whooshly Quincy artwork plus user-triggered Meet Quincy film; Pepys feature illustrations. Short captions state what each execution does.
3. **Principle:** a black typographic pause explaining why a character is a repeatable creative tool.
4. **System:** four colored blocks for create, codify, activate, compound.
5. **Offer:** three clear engagement shapes, no prices.
6. **Why Ankur:** bridge to product development and search work so the site remains one coherent builder/operator story.
7. **Inquiry:** existing validated form workflow with email fallback.

Mobile: hero text leads; art is compressed into a deliberate stacked composition; visual case grids become one column; four-stage system becomes one column at narrow widths; film requires a tap and has a poster. No separate mobile nav item added.

The characters cross two section dividers on scroll. Each crossing plays once, stays within its section, and remains static when reduced motion is requested or JavaScript is unavailable.
