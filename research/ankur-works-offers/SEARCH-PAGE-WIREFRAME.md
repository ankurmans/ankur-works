# Search page wireframe

1. Existing navigation, `Search` selected.
2. Split hero: category + plain-English offer + CTA + owned-product proof note at left; QuoteSweep case-file visual at right.
3. Dark buyer-journey strip: Google result path and AI answer path side by side. Stack on mobile.
4. Existing evidence ledger: QuoteSweep GSC, dated AI answer observations, Pepys PostHog referrals with caveats.
5. Purple proof-to-prospect bridge: visitor site and buyer question.
6. Dark Search-Led GTM system: one compact horizontal discovery-to-conversion visual and four numbered jobs. Stack the visual and jobs on mobile.
7. Light method: five steps, 90-day engagement note, comparison and buyer fit.
8. Caldrin relationship and Product Development cross-link.
9. Final CTA and footer. AI Twin remains shared.

The visual is an explanatory sequence, not a dashboard or claimed funnel measurement. Existing brand color, type, spacing and proof art carry the design.
