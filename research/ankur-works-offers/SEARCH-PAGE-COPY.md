# Search page copy map

Route: `/seo-ai-search/` (unchanged).

- Hero eyebrow: “Search-Led GTM · SEO + AI Search”. H1: “Turn search into a GTM channel.” Subhead explains SEO + AI Search systems around searches, comparisons and recommendations before purchase. CTA: “Talk search”.
- Two buyer journeys: traditional result/click/website and AI answer/sources or recommendations/website or brand search. Close: “Search-Led GTM means designing for both journeys.”
- Owned-product proof: QuoteSweep Google click and AI Overview observations; Pepys ChatGPT referral sessions. Keep their instruments, dates, units and limitations in view.
- System: demand capture, new discovery surfaces, durable assets, commercial measurement. Visual path: discovery → demand capture → trust/sources → conversion.
- Method: map demand → reverse engineer winners → build assets → measure footprint → compound. The 90-day scope is agreed with the client.
- Comparison: SEO work tied to a buyer-demand map and acquisition path. It includes SEO, Google and AI Search; no claim that SEO is obsolete.
- Fit: founder-led and high-value businesses where search can affect pipeline; poor fit for cheap blog volume or vanity rankings.
- Cross-link: Product Development where a prospect needs a tool or product improvement. Caldrin retains its own PE-backed home-services focus.
