# Search-Led GTM positioning

Status: Ankur's offer framing, not an established industry category. Updated 2026-10-01.

**Category:** Search-Led GTM. **Plain-English descriptor:** SEO + AI Search strategy and implementation. **Thesis:** Search belongs in the go-to-market system because commercial discovery can begin with a Google query, an AI answer, a comparison page or a recommendation.

The buyer is a founder or commercial leader with a product or service that should be discovered before a purchase. The outcome sought is qualified demand and a measurable path from discovery to a useful next step. The work: map buyer demand; improve Google and AI discovery surfaces; build pages, research or tools that earn distribution; measure search visibility and on-site actions separately. Revenue and pipeline attribution are discussed only where the client's data supports them.

Search-Led GTM includes technical SEO, content architecture, links, entity clarity and measurement. AI Search adds prompt and source mapping, retrievability, corroboration, and repeated visibility checks. SEO is not obsolete. AI citations are not conversions.

The adjacent Product Development Sprint builds or improves the product. Search-Led GTM builds the discovery and demand path around an existing offer. A project can move between them if the client's need changes; they are not bundled by default.

**Proof boundary:** QuoteSweep GSC clicks grew from 96 in April to 988 in August 2026; that is Google click growth, not leads. Pepys ChatGPT-entry sessions grew from 190 in July to 1,637 in September 2026; that is referral traffic, not citations or attributed revenue. Do not apply either product's result to a prospect.
