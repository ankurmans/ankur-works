# Search-Led GTM offer

## Buyer and fit

Best fit: SaaS, high-value B2B, marketplaces, service businesses and founder-led teams where Search can influence a real buying decision. They have an offer to sell and will share enough data to measure the work. Poor fit: buying cheap blog volume, seeking a vanity ranking, weak search economics, or unwillingness to measure outcomes.

## Four connected jobs

1. **Capture existing demand:** commercial SEO, technical access, landing pages, architecture, internal linking, entity clarity and local discovery when relevant.
2. **Win new discovery surfaces:** comparison and recommendation questions, AI answer sources, retrievable passages, third-party corroboration and consistent entity information.
3. **Build distribution assets:** original evidence, research, useful tools, calculators, datasets, expert material and comparison pages when the buyer need justifies them.
4. **Measure commercial impact:** GSC query and page classes, branded/non-brand separation, commercial-intent clicks, dated AI mention and citation checks, site actions and pipeline only when connected data exists.

## Engagement

Published starting price: $6,000/month; 90-day minimum. Exact scope agreed before work. First review the buyer demand and existing evidence, then ship a bounded set of changes and measure. No ranking, citation, lead or revenue guarantee. CTA: “Talk search” via the current mailto path until the SES form is separately ready.
