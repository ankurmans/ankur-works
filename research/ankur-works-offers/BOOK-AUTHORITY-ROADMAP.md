# Book authority roadmap — Search-Led GTM

This is a research roadmap, not a book draft. Working title: **Search-Led GTM**. Working thesis: Google, AI answers, third-party sources and brand discovery increasingly form one commercial discovery system. The thesis needs evidence across industries before publication.

## Evidence program

1. Define a buyer-question taxonomy and record commercial intent without assuming search volume equals demand.
2. Collect permissioned GSC query/page exports, dated AI answer captures, and on-site actions. Keep measures separate.
3. Build cases where page and source changes are dated. Include failed hypotheses and competing explanations.
4. Test whether original research, tools and comparison assets earn discovery or citations; do not infer causal revenue from visibility alone.
5. Connect to CRM pipeline only with consented, reliable joins; publish uncertainty and exclusions.

## Candidate chapters

- Search inside the go-to-market system
- Mapping demand before buyers know the vendor
- Google pages, AI answers and source ecosystems
- Building assets that deserve distribution
- Measurement that survives a skeptical CFO
- What did not work and why

Articles, research notes, tools and bounded case studies should supply the evidence. Do not force the positioning term into every title.
