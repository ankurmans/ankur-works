# Prompt 1 — Claude Deep Research / Evidence Dossier

You are acting as a senior search, AI visibility, content architecture and digital media analyst.

We are building an unsolicited strategic teardown for:

OUTLEVER  
https://outlever.com/

and its owned publication:

STATE OF BRAND  
https://www.thestateofbrand.com/

Founder:  
Melissa Rosenthal

The finished work will live at:  
https://outlever.ankur.works/

## OBJECTIVE

This is NOT a conventional SEO audit.

We want to produce a highly shareable editorial-style analysis showing:

1. Outlever / State of Brand appear to have built a strong human distribution engine through LinkedIn, executive distribution and newsroom-style publishing.
2. Their organic search footprint appears disproportionately weak relative to their publishing velocity, links, audience and distribution.
3. State of Brand may be creating substantial authority and original information without structurally capturing enough durable search demand or AI retrieval/citation value.
4. Outlever itself appears to have very little commercial organic footprint despite the authority and distribution being generated around the wider ecosystem.
5. There may be a reusable “search + AI capture layer” that can sit on top of the newsroom model Outlever is already building.
6. This is potentially not only an SEO opportunity — it may be a product/module Outlever could deploy across customer newsrooms.

## IMPORTANT POSITIONING

We are NOT trying to say:

> “Your SEO is bad.”

We are trying to say:

> “You solved distribution first. Search and AI capture appear to be the underbuilt second layer.”

The report must give credit before critique.

We are deliberately looking for:

- opportunity
- leakage
- architecture gaps
- search demand not captured
- AI source/citation gaps
- commercial pages Outlever should own
- content clusters where editorial output is competing with itself
- reusable infrastructure that could become part of Outlever’s broader model
