> “Someone understands my business model and found an interesting additional layer.”

NOT:

> “Some SEO agency made a lead-gen landing page about me.”

## CHECK FIVE AREAS

### 1. STRATEGIC CREDIBILITY

Flag:

- weak assumptions
- exaggerated claims
- places where Ahrefs estimates are presented as analytics
- invented intent
- conclusions not supported by evidence
- generic SEO language
- places where we attack instead of complementing their strategy
- a claim of deficient search traffic, AI visibility, or article competition without direct data
- a redirect or canonical-survivor recommendation without GSC query/page and URL-level backlink data
- an AI result table when the direct panel is still pending

### 2. SHAREABILITY

Ask:

- are the first 10 seconds strong?
- does the headline pull?
- is there an obvious screenshot moment in each major third of the page?
- could Melissa share this without embarrassment?
- does it make her / Outlever look intelligent rather than incompetent?
- is the insight bigger than “do SEO”?

### 3. DESIGN

Check:

- desktop
- mobile
- tablet
- typography
- hierarchy
- spacing
- image rendering
- tables
- diagrams
- source footnotes
- CTA

Remove:

- SaaS design clichés
- unnecessary rounded cards
- excessive gradients
- excessive animation
- dashboard cosplay

### 4. TECHNICAL

Check:

- title
- description
- canonical
- OpenGraph
- Twitter
- schema
- heading hierarchy
- source links
- accessibility
- console errors
- broken links
- responsiveness
- layout shift
- print stylesheet
- analytics events

### 5. LEGAL / BRAND PRESENTATION

Ensure it is unmistakably:

**Independent analysis.**

Check that:

- we do not imply endorsement
- we do not impersonate State of Brand
- we do not present first-party claims as independently verified
- screenshots are used in analysis context
- logos / trademarks are not used misleadingly
