## The one-sentence version

A public, evidence-labeled blueprint that connects State of Brand’s live newsroom to evergreen answers, buyer proof, and a bounded pilot.

The first reader is Melissa and the Outlever team; the second is a B2B editorial or growth operator who recognizes the work involved in building a real newsroom. The page should earn a forward because it shows work, not because it makes a louder SEO claim.

## Remarkability check

- **Easy to explain:** the one-sentence version fits in a forwarded note.
- **Visible:** the early evidence index and four-stage map make the reasoning legible without reading the full essay.
- **Worth the recipient’s reputation:** each factual card is bounded by the October 1 capture; the AI state and product layer remain explicitly unproven.
- **Low-friction to share:** the page copies the intended `https://outlever.ankur.works/` URL, and the proposed buyer page is one click from the hero.

## Limits

No reader study, share-rate data, commercial conversion data, or direct AI-answer test has been run. The page remains a local review build and the sending domain had no DNS answer when checked. The design can be evaluated now; audience response and distribution cannot be asserted.
