## Story sections

### The part that is already real

State of Brand makes the newsroom model visible: a live archive, current reporting, and Outlever's first-party account of the system it uses for customers. Our October 1 capture found 216 news URLs in its sitemap and 20 more live news URLs linked from the homepage. That is a minimum observed inventory of 236. It is evidence of publishing output, not evidence of organic traffic, reader loyalty, or commercial performance. [S01] [S02] [S08] [S09]

The distribution model is similarly legible. Outlever's site foregrounds ICPs, executives, and human distribution; Melissa has publicly described interviews and person-to-person sharing as part of the motion. Reported audience numbers exist, but without analytics access they remain first-party or third-party claims. [S01] [S05]

**Pull line:** The archive proves publishing output. The next proof is a route from story to answer to buyer.

### Search is arriving inside the newsroom

The old claim that State of Brand has no search layer is already outdated. Its new “What Is Owned Media in B2B?” and “How B2B Companies Build Owned Media” guides address durable questions directly. The editorial archive also contains earlier owned-media explainers from May and July. Their titles and sections overlap; whether they compete for the same queries is unknown. [S03] [S04] [S06] [S07]

The sitemap tells a narrower, testable story. At capture, 20 live articles linked from the homepage were missing from the XML sitemap, including the two new guides. That is a publishing-system inconsistency, not proof of non-indexing. [S02] [S08] [S09]

**Pull line:** Give each evergreen guide a role, a route from reporting, and an owner who keeps it current.

### One topic, several jobs

The May and July owned-media articles make the case for the category. The September definition guide explains the B2B model; the September build guide shows what an operator would do. Those can be distinct jobs, but the current pages do not make the relationship obvious to a new reader. [S03] [S04] [S06] [S07]

**Proposed system, not a redirect plan:** one maintained definition/hub role; one implementation-guide role; earlier arguments as supporting editorial; contextual paths from each to an Outlever category/proof destination. No URL survivor, merge, or redirect is selected without GSC query/page data and URL-level backlink data.

### What a candidate evergreen hub could say

**Proposed title:** B2B Owned Media: What It Is, How a Newsroom Works, and What to Measure

**Proposed H1:** B2B owned media, explained through the newsroom model

**Draft opening:**

Owned media for a B2B company is a publishing system it controls and an audience chooses to return to. A publication, newsletter, interview series, or event program can all be part of it. The harder part is making those channels worth following when no one is actively shopping. That takes an editorial beat, real reporting, and useful points of view from people who know the market.

A newsroom gives that work a structure. It covers the questions and changes readers care about, brings practitioners into the story, and publishes often enough to become a habit. A maintained evergreen guide has a different job: explain the model, define the terms, and help a reader navigate the reporting behind it. Timely articles can then add new examples and evidence without each one having to reintroduce the category from scratch.

State of Brand already shows both sides in public: frequent editorial coverage and newer owned-media guides. The next move worth testing is a clearer relationship between them. A buyer should be able to move from a breaking story to a durable explanation, see the newsroom in action, and understand what Outlever actually builds. Whether that improves search discovery or commercial response is a measurement question, not a claim this page can make today.

**Proposed H2s:** What counts as owned media in B2B; How a brand newsroom operates; Publication versus company blog; Distribution without renting every impression; What to measure; Build in-house or with a partner; State of Brand as a live example.

**Editorial links:** link relevant articles as examples from each section; link the how-to guide for implementation; link a proposed Outlever category page for the service model. This is candidate copy, not a published replacement. [S01] [S03] [S04]

### The missing public bridge

State of Brand is a useful demonstration of Outlever's offer. Yet the inspected Outlever homepage does not point to it, and the commercial site exposes no internally linked service or case-study destination beyond the homepage/contact anchor. The commercial opportunity is a clearer route from “this newsroom is interesting” to “this is what Outlever would build for us.” That is an architecture observation, not a conversion analysis. [S01] [S09]

The copy uses Outlever's public positioning as a starting point and would require the company's review before appearing on its own site. [S01] [S09]

### Live AI visibility panel pending

No direct ChatGPT Search, Google AI, or Perplexity answers were captured in Phase 1. A panel should use the fixed 15 prompts in the dossier across available engines, with the exact prompt, date, locale/account context, full answer, named brands, cited URLs, and repeated-run variance recorded. This page shows the method and an empty measurement state, never result-shaped placeholders.

### A layer to test, not a gap to declare

The proposed Search + AI Capture Layer maps durable buyer questions, assigns clear evergreen roles, routes fresh reporting to those pages, connects proof to commercial destinations, and measures search and AI discovery. It would sit beneath the newsroom's editorial judgment. Outlever may already do parts of this internally; the public evidence does not establish otherwise. [S01] [S09]

### A bounded first pilot

**Week 1:** Establish GSC query/page and URL-level backlink baselines; select a cluster only after reviewing them; run the initial AI prompt panel if access permits.

**Week 2:** Build a candidate evergreen hub and contextual story links, preserving existing article URLs until evidence supports change.

**Week 3:** Design one Outlever category/proof destination with the team and connect it to State of Brand; publish only with Outlever's approval.

**Week 4:** Check implementation, query-to-page mapping, early impressions, and AI panel variance. Continue measurement beyond four weeks before judging outcomes.

**CTA:** We built this because the model is interesting. If the team wants to test the next layer, the pilot is designed. Let's compare notes.
