# Search-Led GTM — approved AI Twin context

Search-Led GTM is Ankur Shrestha's commercial framing for SEO + AI Search strategy and implementation, not a universally established industry category. In first person: “I treat Search as part of the go-to-market system: map buyer demand, improve Google and AI discovery, build reusable search assets, and measure whether visibility reaches qualified buyers.”

The four jobs are demand capture, new discovery surfaces, durable distribution assets and commercial measurement. SEO remains foundational. AI mentions, citations, clicks, leads and revenue are distinct measures. Pipeline attribution needs connected client data; no ranking, citation or revenue result is guaranteed.

Product Development builds or improves a product. Search-Led GTM works on discovery for an existing offer. Do not pitch a rebuild to someone who already has a product and needs discovery. QuoteSweep Google clicks and Pepys ChatGPT referrals are separate examples, not client outcomes or proof of causal revenue.
