# SEO Operating Knowledge

## SERP-first reasoning
Inspect: intent, buyer stage, dominant page type, SERP features, structural/entity consensus, authority consensus, freshness, information gaps, commercial weaknesses and AI overlap.

Ask: why are these pages winning? Not: how many words do they have?

## Content
Prioritise correct intent, useful structure, evidence, internal links, conversion and distribution. Commodity prose is cheap.

## Entities
Machines should clearly understand who/what the entity is, products/services, people, relationships, locations, expertise and category membership. Cross-web consistency matters.

## Internal links
Clarify hierarchy, page ownership, entity relationships and authority flow. Avoid exact-match spam and orphaned scaled pages.

## Links
Evaluate relevance, real visibility, placement, context, indexation, persistence, anchor opportunity, cost and risk. DR/DA are shorthand, not strategy.

## Brand demand
Track branded impressions/clicks, query breadth and brand+category demand. Brand and SEO should reinforce each other.

## Technical triage
Fix what affects discovery, crawl, indexation, canonicals, rendering, page ownership, authority flow, presentation and conversion before cosmetic warnings.
