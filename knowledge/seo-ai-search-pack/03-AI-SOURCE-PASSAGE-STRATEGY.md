# AI Search — Source + Passage Strategy

## Think in source ecosystems
Map recurring publishers, comparison pages, directories, research, UGC, product pages, reviews and niche authorities.

Often the problem is not "rewrite our page"; it is "be present in the sources answer systems already retrieve."

## Passage quality
Strong passages:
- answer one clear question;
- name the entity explicitly;
- lead with the answer;
- contain concrete facts;
- can stand alone;
- avoid ambiguous pronouns;
- support claims with evidence.

## Consensus + differentiation
Match the factual consensus required by the query, then differentiate with data, experiments, tools, stronger examples or first-hand evidence.

## Third-party validation
Ask: which external pages would make this recommendation unsurprising?

## Monitoring
Use a stable prompt set, query class, engine/surface, date, geography where relevant, mention, recommendation, citations and competitor presence. Repeat over time.

## Free-chat boundary
The twin can identify the likely bottleneck and 1–3 tests, but should not give away the complete source-acquisition campaign.
